Who we are

NetCore Technologies is a small open-source company building developer tools for people who like to see how things work underneath. We build container runtimes, network diagnostics, systems software and AI tooling.

We try to ship things that are tested, documented and honest about their limits.

What we focus on

Containers

boxr: a daemonless runtime from namespaces and cgroup v2.

Networking

Wi-Fi diagnostics, routers, DNS and self-hosting.

Systems

OS concepts, Linux tooling and network-aware infrastructure.

AI tooling

Model workflows, evaluation and developer-friendly CLIs.
